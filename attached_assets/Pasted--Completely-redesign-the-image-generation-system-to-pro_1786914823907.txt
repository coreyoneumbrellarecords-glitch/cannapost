"Completely redesign the image generation system to produce a new style of promotional graphics. Do not change the AGCO compliance system, caption generation, Claude API integration, or any other existing functionality. Only replace how the promotional images are generated and styled.

THE NEW IMAGE STYLE
Every generated image must look like a high end luxury graphic design piece created by a professional cannabis brand designer. The style is bold illustrated 3D art with the strain or product name as the centerpiece of the composition. Think luxury product packaging meets fantasy art meets high fashion editorial. No photorealistic cannabis buds or plant imagery at all. The product is communicated through thematic artistic elements specific to the strain name and flavor profile.

CORE DESIGN RULES
The strain or product name must be rendered as large dimensional 3D typography that is part of the artwork itself, not just text overlaid on top. The letters should have texture, depth, and material quality such as glossy candy, molten gold, chrome metal, crystallized sugar, liquid silk, or holographic foil depending on the strain theme. The typography IS the hero of the image.

Every image must have a rich immersive background that tells the story of the strain through color, texture, and thematic elements. The background is never generic. It is always custom to that specific strain name and flavor profile.

Real world objects that match the strain name or flavor should appear as artistic illustrated elements floating throughout the composition. For example Blueberry strains get real photorealistic blueberries integrated into the flowing abstract art. Pink Goo gets candy swirls, lollipops, and glossy dripping pink textures. Gelato gets ice cream swirls and Italian dessert elements. Sour Diesel gets electric acid yellow liquid and industrial chrome pipes. Wedding Cake gets elegant fondant textures and rose gold ribbon. The thematic objects should feel like they exist in the same artistic world as the typography.

Abstract flowing elements such as silk ribbons, liquid chrome streams, smoke wisps, holographic ribbons, aurora waves, or crystal formations should swirl and flow throughout the composition connecting all elements together.
